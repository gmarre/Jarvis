3. Customer account

The purchase journey must remain accessible to unidentified visitors up to the payment step.

The customer must be able to create an account with an email address and a password compliant with the group security policy. Confirmation is done through a link sent by email.

The system must allow sign-in through an external identity provider (Google, Apple).

Three failed sign-in attempts lock the account for 15 minutes. Early unlocking is possible from the back-office.

Authentication response time must stay under 800 ms at the 95th percentile.

The address book accepts up to ten delivery addresses per customer account.
